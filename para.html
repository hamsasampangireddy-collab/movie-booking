Movie Summary The Paradise (2026) is a Telugu-language period action-drama film directed by Srikanth Odela. Set in the gritty slums of 1980s Secunderabad, the movie follows the story of Jadal, the leader of a marginalized, nomadic tribal community who rallies his people to fight against systemic discrimination, corruption, and an oppressive local regime to secure their identity and citizenship rights.
