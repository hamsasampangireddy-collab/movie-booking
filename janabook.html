Movie Summary The Protagonist: Vijay plays Vetri Kondan (or Vetri), a fearless former police inspector. The Father-Daughter Bond: Vetri takes in and raises Viji (played by Mamitha Baiju), the traumatized daughter of a deceased friend, training and encouraging her to join the Indian Army. The Conflict: Their personal lives collide with a much larger threat when they must stop John Himmler (Bobby Deol), an international arms dealer and extremist executing a chaos-inducing technological/communal plot against India's democracy, alongside local corrupt politicians like Prakash Raj.
