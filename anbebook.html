Anbe dayana

About movie

Movie Summary • The Protagonists: Chirumamilla Sita Krishna (Pari Elavazhagan), a traditional and sports-coaching Telugu man, lives in the vibrant Perambur neighborhood of Chennai. He falls in love with Magic Diana D'Souza (Ramya Ranganathan), a free-spirited, liberal Anglo-Indian IT worker.
• The Conflict: Krishna's family—particularly his caste-obsessed, upper-caste Andhra mother, Sarala (Roja Selvamani), and his railway-employee father, Bhaskar (Chetan Kadambi)—expects him to marry within their community. Krishna panics over Magic's modern lifestyle, clothes, and independent nature, worrying how his family will react.
