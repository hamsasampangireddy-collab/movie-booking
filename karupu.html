Karupu

About movie

Movie Summary Karuppu (2026) is a Tamil-language fantasy action-drama film directed by RJ Balaji and starring Suriya. The story follows a poor father and daughter from Kerala who travel to Tamil Nadu for a life-saving liver transplant, only to have their treatment funds stolen. Trapped in a heavily corrupted judicial system run by a ruthless, predatory lawyer named Baby Kannan, the girl tragically passes away due to endless court delays. Heartbroken, the father performs a ritual invoking the village guardian deity, Vettai Karuppu Saamy. The deity answers the prayer, taking human form as a mortal lawyer named Saravanan.
