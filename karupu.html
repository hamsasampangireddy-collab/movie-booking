Karupu

summary about movie

Movie Summary The story revolves around Sukumaran (played by Indrans) and his young daughter Binu (played by Anagha Maya Ravi), who travel from Kerala to Chennai for Binu's life-saving liver transplant. They carry a substantial amount of gold meant to fund the surgery, but it is stolen during their journey. When they seek help from the police, they are trapped in systemic exploitation. They are told they must file a "Return of Property" petition in the Seven Wells district court to get their gold back. The local court is run like a personal fiefdom by Baby Kannan (played by RJ Balaji), a highly predatory and corrupt lawyer who deliberately drags out the cases of helpless individuals to extort them for money. Out of sheer hopelessness and desperation, Sukumaran invokes Vettai Karuppu, the ancient local guardian deity. Answering his devotee's prayer, the deity descends to Earth. Taking a human form, the god poses as a newly transferred advocate named Saravanan (played by Suriya).
