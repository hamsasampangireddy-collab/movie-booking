Movie Summary The 2026 Tamil film DC (Das & Chandra) is a gritty, high-octane romantic action-thriller that adapts the classic Devadas character into a modern, violent Western-style revenge saga. Directed by Arun Matheswaran and produced by Sun Pictures, the film famously marks the lead acting debut of blockbuster director Lokesh Kanagaraj.
• Devadas (Lokesh Kanagaraj): Introduced as "Dv Das," he is a rogue outlaw and the leader of a heavily armed rebel faction. Devadas is a deeply flawed, alcoholic vigilante who spends his time robbing powerful syndicates to distribute wealth back to oppressed communities.
