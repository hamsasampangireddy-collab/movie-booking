Enna vilai

About movie

Movie Summary The story is set against the coastal backdrop of Rameswaram. Thavasi, a humble local fisherman who occasionally dives for coins to make ends meet, finds a valuable gold pendant/medallion washed ashore on the seabed. What initially looks like a life-changing blessing quickly morphs into an absolute nightmare. A corrupt local policeman frames him for an interstate jewelry theft case. The police continually shift their charges just to keep him trapped as the prime suspect. This thrusts Thavasi into an intense, systemic legal battle. The conflict deepens into a struggle to clear his name, safeguard his family, and protect his fundamental human dignity against a powerful, compromised administrative machinery.
