Movie Summary • The Plot: Set in a nostalgic mid-2010s backdrop, a carefree 15-year-old student named Praveen stumbles through high school with one singular mission: finding true love before his school days end. His chaotic crushes and family clashes anchor this relatable comedy-drama. • Key Actors: Ken Karunas stars as the misguided yet charming lead Praveen. The film features breakthrough leading performances from rising actress Anishma Anilkumar (as the class leader Kanakavalli), Meenakshi Dinesh, and Hindi television favorite Priyanshi Yadav in her highly anticipated Tamil cinema debut.
