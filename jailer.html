Movie Summary Jailer 2 is a 2026 Indian Tamil-language action-comedy thriller starring Superstar Rajinikanth. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar and produced by Sun Pictures, the film released worldwide on October 15, 2026. It is the highly anticipated sequel to the 2023 blockbuster Jailer.
🎬 Plot & Synopsis The story follows retired jailer Tiger Muthuvel Pandian (Rajinikanth). When a dangerous criminal syndicate expands its network and threatens his family's peace, Pandian goes undercover as a driver. Operating strategically from the shadows, he uses his lethal skills one final time to take down ruthless new enemies.
