I'm Game

About Movie

"I'm Game" (2026) is a Malayalam fantasy-action thriller starring Dulquer Salmaan as Dan John, an arrogant, wealthy man obsessed with cricket betting and gambling. After landing in severe debt, a brutal accident leaves him requiring a hand transplant. The plot takes a supernatural turn when the newly transplanted hand begins acting on its own, attempting to assassinate a powerful businessman. Dan discovers the hand belongs to a murdered victim seeking vengeance. Directed by Nahas Hidhayath, the high-octane movie blends action and karma. Following its September theatrical release, it streams on JioHotstar starting November 6, 2026.
