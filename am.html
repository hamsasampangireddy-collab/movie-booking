I'm Game

summary about movie

Movie Summary I'm Game (2026) is a high-concept Malayalam fantasy action-thriller that uniquely blends a crime thriller with body-horror and psychological fantasy elements. Directed by Nahas Hidhayath and backed by Dulquer Salmaan's Wayfarer Films, the film has generated significant discussion for its unusual premise.
