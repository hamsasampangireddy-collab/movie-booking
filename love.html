Love Today

summary about movie

Movie Summary The story follows a young, tech-savvy couple, Uthaman Pradeep and Nikitha Shastri, who want to get married. However, Nikitha’s suspicious father, Venu Sastri, proposes a strict challenge before giving his blessing: the couple must exchange their mobile phones for 24 hours. What starts as a seemingly simple test quickly descends into hilarious chaos and high-stakes drama. As they dig through each other’s WhatsApp messages, Instagram DMs, and call histories, hidden secrets, past crushes, and double identities are exposed. The film explores whether modern relationships can survive absolute digital transparency.
