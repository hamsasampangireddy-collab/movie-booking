Modha rathri

About movie

Movie Summary Modha Rathiri is a 2026 Tamil rural comedy-drama directed by Raja Karuppasamy. The story revolves around Marudhu (Rishikanth) and Muthuselvi (Anishma Anilkumar), who are pressured into a forced, arranged marriage by their families. Neither wants the wedding; Marudhu is harboring grief from his past, while Muthuselvi is secretly in love with someone else. On their wedding night, Muthuselvi confesses her feelings. Rather than reacting with anger, Marudhu handles it maturely, causing the couple to escape the celebration. Their sudden disappearance throws both families and the village into absolute chaos, sparking a night filled with hilarious misunderstandings, family brawls, and unexpected emotional healing
