Modha rathri

summary about movie

Movie Summary Modha Rathri (transl. First Night) is a 2026 Indian Tamil-language comedy-drama film written and directed by Raja Karuppasamy in his directorial debut. Produced by Mythri Movie Makers, the film stars newcomers Rishikanth and Anishma Anilkumar in the lead roles, alongside a supporting cast including Shelly Kishore, Chetan, and Bagavathi Perumal. Set entirely over the course of a single night, the plot follows two strangers whose forced marriage spirals into hilarious family chaos after an unexpected wedding night confession prompts them to flee. Released over the Milad un-Nabi holiday weekend, the film became a box office sleeper hit, earning widespread critical acclaim for its witty dialogue, performances, and clean family-centric writing
