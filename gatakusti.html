Gata Kusti

About movie

Movie Summary Movie Plot Summary The story revolves around Veera (Vishnu Vishal), a chauvinistic man with highly specific criteria for his future wife: she must be uneducated, timid, submissive, and possess long hair. Through a series of misdirections by family members, he unknowingly marries Keerthi (Aishwarya Lekshmi), who is actually an independent, well-educated, state-level champion wrestler with short hair. Keerthi puts on a submissive facade with hair extensions to keep the marriage intact. The crux of the movie deals with the hilarious and emotional fallout when Veera discovers her true identity, forcing him to unlearn traditional gender roles and confront his preconceived notions of masculinity.
