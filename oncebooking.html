Once More

About movie

Movie Summary Set in 1995 near Madurai, Tamil Nadu, the story follows two inseparable childhood friends, Karthi and Subbu. Karthi is from an oppressed caste, while Subbu belongs to a wealthy, dominant-caste family. Subbu’s family is led by his horrific, domineering father, Viraa, who enforces rigid rules of caste honor, machismo, and violent brotherhood. While Karthi grows into a "tough guy" to survive, Subbu grows increasingly sensitive, vulnerable, and deeply disconnected from his family's violent expectations.
